# Demo stories

103 visualised demo stories. Open the site, filter by function, industry, persona
or tier, and click a story to play it full screen. Click or use the arrow keys to
move through it; press Escape or Back to return to the list.

**Visualised — not a live system.** Every company, person and figure shown is
invented. Nothing here is a real customer, a real competitor or a real record.

Static build. Story asset paths are prefixed with `/demo-stories/`, and the
webfonts are shared from `/demo-stories/fonts/` rather than bundled into each
story.
